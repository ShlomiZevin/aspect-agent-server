## 3. The rewrite

**Positioning call:** Do not claim “AI OS” yet. It implies third-party builders, an app ecosystem and distribution you do not have today. The defensible version is stronger: **One foundation. Anything on top.** Make that Slide 3; it answers the services objection without overclaiming.

If challenged: “I am not claiming an app ecosystem today. I mean the architecture: the AI learns the business once, then builds reports, screens, automations and systems on that same foundation. Today, the AI is the builder. Opening that layer to others is a direction, not the claim.”
